# Easy Maths tracker: project state

## Location and architecture

The tracker lives at `/tracker` in this website repository. The larger `study_app/lc-maths-study-app` repository was inspected but is unchanged: its `src/types.ts`, `src/data/papers.ts`, and `src/utils/progress.ts` describe two original sample papers and device-local answer checks, not dated study-method attempts. The tracker deliberately uses its own stable paper/part IDs and attempt model. No cross-repository runtime import exists.

`app/tracker/catalogue.ts` owns paper and part metadata. `app/tracker/model.ts` owns the scoring configuration and calculations. `app/tracker/auth.ts` handles passwords and sessions; `app/tracker/server.ts` handles access checks. API routes handle account setup, sign-in, attempts, tutor-created students, and exports. `app/tracker/tracker.tsx` is the student/tutor interface. The D1 migration is `drizzle/0000_short_goliath.sql`.

The tracker opens with **Log attempt** and **See progress**. Logging selects year, paper, sitting when applicable, question number, then a valid subpart before opening a full-screen attempt form. Progress summarizes all saved attempts, recent history, and parts to revisit. Tutor account controls are under **Manage students**; the tutor's own practice uses the same flow as a student.

After a new attempt, a separate success screen acknowledges the work equally at all five levels. Its second line is a factual update about this week's practice day, a new part, or a repeat attempt. Editing an attempt and retrying an existing submission ID do not trigger a new-attempt celebration. The screen offers **Log another question** and **See progress**.

The weekly goal defaults to three practice days and can be changed by the account owner to two or four on Progress. One or more attempts on a distinct Europe/Dublin calendar day count as one practice day in the Monday-to-Sunday week. The level, marks, and number of attempts on that day do not change goal credit. Backdated edits and deletions recompute the count from the current attempt history. The goal preference is stored on `tracker_users.weekly_goal_days`; `drizzle/0001_brown_goliath.sql` adds it to existing accounts with a default of three. The browser demo keeps its goal choice in localStorage.

The primary weekly target is now 20 logged parts, roughly half a Higher Level paper; 12 and 30 are optional targets. Each saved attempt in the current Europe/Dublin Monday-to-Sunday week counts once regardless of method level; new-part attempts and revisits are shown separately. Counts are recomputed after edits and deletions. The practice-day goal remains available under a secondary disclosure on Progress. `tracker_users.weekly_part_goal` defaults to 20 via additive migration `drizzle/0002_nasty_malice.sql`. In the question picker, each part shows its latest dated attempt level and total attempt count, or "Not attempted". Starting a new attempt never copies the previous level.

The picker styles past results by latest level: Level 1 is neutral, Levels 2–4 gain increasingly distinct warm and green accents, and Level 5 has a prominent green achievement badge. The badge always includes a text label, so colour is not the only cue. This visual treatment does not change the equal-level save celebration or weekly goal credit.

Progress now leads with three concrete measures. **Paper-worth of practice** is total saved attempts divided into groups of 35 trackable parts; repeats count, and it is explicitly not an actual completed paper. **Parts improved** counts distinct parts whose later attempt exceeded their previous personal best level; a 1→2 gain counts, while falling and returning to an old best does not add another gain. The monthly figure counts distinct improved parts and total level steps during the Europe/Dublin calendar month. **Done without help** counts distinct parts whose latest dated attempt is Level 4 or 5; it can go down if a later attempt needs help. Deletions and edits recompute these figures from the remaining attempt history. A new best is also noted after saving that attempt. Progress suggests one part whose latest level is 1–3 as a next step.

The paper-volume card displays total saved parts and the estimate `attempts / 35` to two decimal places. When no part has improved yet, or none is currently at Level 4–5, the corresponding headline card shows a short next-action prompt instead of a zero. Topic cards similarly avoid a zero headline for parts done without help.

The topic view uses `structure-reviewed` parts with checked topic IDs: the 2026 normal papers and all 22 Edco book papers. It shows latest level counts and parts done without help in each topic. Multi-topic parts appear in each relevant topic but only once in overall counts. No exam-mark estimate is inferred from method levels. The old average-level and attempt trend figures remain under **More practice details** so their changing question mix is not mistaken for an exam prediction.

Each reviewed topic card also shows **Average logged level** = sum of method levels from every logged attempt on tagged parts divided by the number of those attempts. Repeats therefore contribute again to this average. Its denominator is displayed; the five level-count chips still use each distinct part's latest attempt only. A multi-topic attempt contributes once to each tagged topic's average. This remains a study-method indicator, not a mark prediction.

## Configuration and deployment

1. From `easymaths-ie`, run `npm install` if needed, then `npm run tracker:db:local`. This creates and migrates the local D1 database. The command is safe to rerun.
2. Run `npm run dev`. Open the printed localhost URL with `/tracker/login` and create your tutor username, name, and a password of at least 12 characters. The first-account form is available only on local development hosts while no tutor exists. `/tracker` redirects there when signed out.
3. The tutor can record attempts under **My practice**. In **My students**, the tutor can also create a student's display name, username, and password. Give them those details directly. They sign in at `/tracker/login`. Tutor and student attempts are stored in local D1 and survive browser reloads.
4. `/tracker?demo=1` remains a separate browser-only demo. It uses localStorage and does not use accounts.

The tracker is live at `https://easymaths.ie/tracker` on the existing Netlify site. The 26 October 2026 release of PR #1 replaced the static Netlify build with a server build and a Firestore adapter. Its Firebase project is `easymaths-ie`. The owner created a tutor account and confirmed attempt persistence on the deploy preview; the same Firestore database serves production. Production smoke checks passed, but the owner still needs to confirm sign-in and the saved attempt on the live URL. The Netlify badge was removed without changing hosts or DNS. A separate owner-only ChatGPT Site has a deployed copy with its own D1 and `TRACKER_SETUP_EMAIL`; its accounts and attempts are separate from local D1 and Firestore. See `TRACKER_LIVE_HANDOFF.md`. Never put credentials in `NEXT_PUBLIC_` variables.

For code checks run `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, and `npm test`. The public challenge and grinds pages remain in place.

## Accounts and permissions

New passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256 at 100,000 iterations and a random salt, the maximum accepted by the hosted runtime. Existing local 600,000-iteration hashes remain readable locally. A random 14-day session token is stored only as a hash in local D1 or hosted Firestore; its cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and Secure on HTTPS. Login throttles repeated failures. Students can create, edit, delete, and see only attempts tied to their own ID. The tutor can record their own attempts, read and export assigned students, and cannot alter a student's attempts. Every attempt mutation checks the signed-in ID and attempt ownership; tutor reads check the assignment record. Browser mutations also require a same-origin `Origin` header. The public pages do not require tracker sign-in.

Signed-in users can change their password from the tracker home screen; this ends all of their sessions. Tutors can reset a password for any assigned student from Manage students; this ends that student's sessions and keeps their practice history. Students who forget their password should ask their tutor for a reset. Tutor account recovery after a forgotten password is not yet self-service; keep the tutor password in a password manager.

## Scoring and summaries

`easy-maths-five-levels-v1` follows `Intro lesson sheets/EasyMaths-Study-System.pdf`: 1 Copy the solution, 2 Build with the solution, 3 Use notes or examples, 4 Answer independently, 5 Answer at exam pace (correctly, in time). Higher means less help. This is an ordinal study-method indicator, never a predicted grade.

Each save is a distinct dated attempt; editing keeps its ID, while deletion removes only that attempt. The **all-attempt** mean counts every selected attempt. The **latest-per-part** mean counts the latest practice date per part (created date breaks ties). Coverage is distinct attempted parts divided by available structure-reviewed parts in the selected paper/topic scope. The trend groups all attempts by practice date and plots their mean, with counts. Marks use `sum(earned)/sum(maximum)` over all selected attempts with both values, separately from method score. Blank marks are excluded; a recorded zero is included. A mark with an unknown maximum is stored but excluded from the ratio until completed. Multi-topic parts can count in more than one topic summary, while counting once in the overall paper summary. Revisit shows parts with latest level 1–3.

## Catalogue and source gaps

The Edco contents photographs supplied on 25 October 2026 confirm the **22-paper set**: normal Paper 1 and 2 in 2018–2025, plus deferred Paper 1 and 2 in 2022–2024. The catalogue has **1049 structure-reviewed leaf parts**: 95 in the 2026 normal papers and 954 in the Edco book papers. Parts are ordered by question and subpart, with parent prompts represented by their leaves only. IDs include course, level, year, sitting, paper and part. A source PDF path and review status accompany every part. Individual maximum marks are `null`: the papers show whole-question totals, not verified leaf allocations. The app does not display source question text or book pages.

The prototype repository now has **75** PDFs under `data/papers/`, matching its manifest. The missing 2023 normal Paper 2 was copied from `~/Downloads/23_ppr2.pdf` after checking its title and paper code. Both missing 2019 normal papers were downloaded from the official SEC URLs in the manifest and checked against their PDF title pages. These three PDFs are local sources and remain ignored by the study-app Git repository. The local folder contains no marking schemes.

The supplied `Paper 1 Topics.xlsx` and `Paper 2 Topics.xlsx` cover the same 22 papers. `Intro lesson sheets/EasyMaths-Topic-Priorities.pdf` says its 2018–2025 figures were calculated from Edco past papers. The workbooks are classification hints, not a question-part inventory or verified leaf mark allocations. Their shorthand, such as `Q10d(iii),e`, is expanded against real PDF part codes before use.

Run `python3 scripts/audit_tracker_papers.py` to reproduce `content/edco-2018-2025-candidates.json`. It inventories all 22 source PDFs, hashes them, records raw topic hints, and extracts **954 candidate leaf parts** with source pages. `python3 scripts/check_tracker_structure.py` confirms that extraction in raw reading order matches the saved part order for all 22 PDF hashes; this is a check, not visual certification. A repeated answer-box label in 2022 deferred Paper 1 Q6(a) is deduplicated. Run `python3 scripts/map_tracker_topics.py` to reproduce `content/edco-2018-2025-topic-review.json`: all 954 parts have topic IDs after 74 PDF-text-checked corrections in `content/edco-topic-corrections.json`. Seven deliberate two-topic overlaps remain. Four source-reference errors are recorded: 2018 normal P1 Q6(c), 2022 normal P1 Q2(ii), and 2024 normal P2 Q9(a)(iv) and Q10(a)(iv); the cited parts do not exist, except Q2(ii), which the PDF resolves as Q2(b)(ii). All 22 papers' extracted part lists and topic references were checked against PDF text, with targeted prompt review where the workbook grouping was misleading. This is not full visual certification of every printed page or a review of leaf marks. The older 2018–2020 papers have nine questions, while later papers have ten; do not interpret the workbook's fixed `coverage_vs_380` denominator as the actual paper mark total for those older years.

To verify the catalogue, review each candidate against its official PDF and the Edco contents, then update its review status while preserving the stable ID; do not change existing IDs when reclassifying topics. Expand workbook shorthand only where the paper structure makes it unambiguous; flag the rest. Add only verified leaf mark allocations. Existing attempt rows retain their part IDs across metadata changes. A tutor's JSON export contains schema version, scale, catalogue, and attempts; CSV exports attempts. Exports require the same assignment check as the tutor view.
